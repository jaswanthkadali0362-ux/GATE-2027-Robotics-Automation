# IISc RBCCPS: Complete Admission, Research Areas & Interview Guide

**Centre:** Robert Bosch Centre for Cyber-Physical Systems (RBCCPS), Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore  
**Key Programs:** M.Tech in Robotics & Autonomous Systems / M.Tech in Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) / M.Tech (Research) / Ph.D.

---

## 🏛️ About RBCCPS at IISc
RBCCPS is India's premier interdisciplinary research centre dedicated to:
1. **Robotics & Autonomous Systems**: Mobile robots, autonomous ground & aerial vehicles (drones/UAVs), robotic manipulation, SLAM, path planning.
2. **Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS)**: IoT networks, real-time embedded systems, cyber-security of physical infrastructure, smart grids, connected mobility.
3. **Control & AI for Robotics**: Reinforcement learning for robotics, model predictive control (MPC), state estimation (Kalman filters), multi-agent coordination.

---

## 🎯 Admission Process for M.Tech / M.Tech (Research)

### 1. Shortlisting through GATE Score
- Candidates with a top percentile in **GATE RA (Robotics & Automation)**, **GATE ME, EE, EC, IN, CS, or DA** are shortlisted.
- Having a strong score in **GATE RA** directly showcases that your foundational preparation aligns 100% with RBCCPS core coursework!

### 2. Written Test / Coding Round (if applicable)
- **Mathematical Foundations**: Linear Algebra (Rank, Nullspace, Eigenvalues, SVD), Vector Calculus (Gradients, Jacobians, Hessians), Probability (Bayes theorem, Gaussian distributions).
- **Programming & Algorithms**: Python / C++ coding problems testing arrays, recursion, BFS/DFS, graph traversals, and simple algorithmic problem solving.

### 3. Technical Interview Rounds
The interview panel typically consists of 2-3 IISc professors who test conceptual depth and problem-solving intuition rather than rote memorization:

#### Common Interview Focus Areas:
1. **Linear Algebra & Transformations**:
   - *"Explain why rotation matrices are orthogonal with determinant +1."*
   - *"How do you parameterize 3D rotations without gimbal lock? (Euler angles vs. Quaternions vs. Axis-Angle)."*
   - *"Given a 2-DOF planar arm, derive its Jacobian matrix and identify singular configurations."*
2. **Control Systems & State Space**:
   - *"How do you design a state-feedback controller? What is the physical meaning of controllability and observability?"*
   - *"Explain how a PID controller works intuitively. When would you need the Derivative (D) term versus the Integral (I) term?"*
3. **State Estimation & Filtering**:
   - *"What is the intuition behind a Kalman Filter? How does it fuse noisy sensor data (e.g. IMU + Wheel Encoders)?"*
4. **Data Structures & Code Walkthrough**:
   - *"How does the $A^*$ algorithm find the shortest collision-free path in a grid?"*
   - *"Write pseudo-code for Breadth-First Search (BFS) and explain why it guarantees shortest path in unweighted graphs."*
5. **Past Projects & Motivation**:
   - Discussion on your undergraduate final year project, robotics club experience, ROS / Gazebo / MATLAB simulations, or hardware implementations.

## 🚀 Recommended RBCCPS Preparation Strategy
1. **Master the Mathematical Rigor**: Don't just memorize formulas; understand the geometry behind transformation matrices, projections, eigenvalues, and Taylor series.
2. **Hands-On Python / Simulation**: Write Python scripts to simulate kinematics, control loops, and path planning algorithms (we will include these in this repository).
3. **Clear Conceptual Foundations in Part A**: Master Sensors, Actuators, Digital Circuits, and Kinematics.
