# Curated Textbooks, Free NPTEL Video Lectures & Resources for GATE RA

Don't worry that the **RA paper is newly introduced**! Every topic in the GATE RA syllabus is taken directly from established undergraduate curricula across Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, and Computer Science.

Here is the exact mapping of **Top Standard Textbooks**, **Free NPTEL Video Courses (by IIT/IISc Professors)**, and **Online Resources**.

---

## 1. Core Robotics (Spatial Transformations, Kinematics, Dynamics, Jacobians)

### 📖 Standard Textbooks
1. **Introduction to Robotics: Mechanics and Control** – *John J. Craig* (The Holy Grail for DH parameters, rotation matrices, forward/inverse kinematics, Jacobians).
2. **Robot Modeling and Control** – *Mark W. Spong, Seth Hutchinson, M. Vidyasagar* (Unmatched mathematical clarity, state-space & trajectory control).
3. **Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control** – *Kevin M. Lynch and Frank C. Park* (Free online PDF + free Northwestern Coursera video series).

### 🎥 Free High-Yield Video Courses
* **Robotics** – *Prof. Dilip Kumar Pratihar (IIT Kharagpur)* | [NPTEL YouTube Playlist](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112105249)
* **Mechanisms and Robot Kinematics** – *Prof. Anirvan Dasgupta (IIT Kharagpur)* | [NPTEL Course](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112105236)
* **Introduction to Robotics** – *Prof. Ashish Dutta (IIT Kanpur) & Prof. Asokan T (IIT Madras)* | [NPTEL Playlist](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112106209)
* **Modern Robotics Playlist** – *Prof. Kevin Lynch (Northwestern University)* (Available free on YouTube, search "Modern Robotics Northwestern").

## 2. Basics of Mechatronics, Sensors & Actuators

### 📖 Standard Textbooks
1. **Mechatronics: Electronic Control Systems in Mechanical and Electrical Engineering** – *W. Bolton* (Sensors, signal conditioning, actuators, PLCs).
2. **Measurement Systems: Application and Design** – *Ernest O. Doebelin* (Detailed transducer working principles, LVDT, strain gauges, piezoelectric).
3. **Electric Machinery and Drives / Electric Motors** – *P.S. Bimbhra* or *Stephen J. Chapman* (DC motors, Stepper, BLDC torque-speed curves).

### 🎥 Free High-Yield Video Courses

## 4. Python Programming & Data Structures

## 6. Optional Part B Streams

### For B1 (Electrical Stream)
* **Control Systems**:
  * Book: *Modern Control Engineering* by Katsuhiko Ogata / *Control Systems Engineering* by Norman S. Nise.
  * Video: *Control Systems* by Prof. Ramkrishna Pasumarthy (IIT Madras) or Brian Douglas (YouTube).
* **Signals & Systems**:
  * Book: *Signals and Systems* by Alan V. Oppenheim.
  * Video: *Signals and Systems* by Prof. K.S. Venkatesh (IIT Kanpur).
* **Analog & Embedded**:
  * Book: *Microelectronic Circuits* by Sedra & Smith; *The 8051 / ARM Cortex Embedded Systems* by Mazidi.

### For B2 (Mechanical Stream)
* **Strength of Materials (SOM)**:
  * Book: *Mechanics of Materials* by Beer, Johnston & Dewolf / Gere & Timoshenko.
  * Video: *Strength of Materials* by Prof. S.P. Harsha (IIT Roorkee).
* **Theory of Machines (Kinematics & Dynamics)**:
  * Book: *Theory of Machines and Mechanisms* by John J. Uicker, Gordon R. Pennock, Joseph E. Shigley / *SS Rattan*.
* **Machine Design**:
  * Book: *Mechanical Engineering Design* by Shigley / *V.B. Bhandari*.

## 7. How to Leverage Past Year Questions (PYQs)
Since RA 2027 is a new paper, practice PYQs from sibling branches:
1. **GATE Mechanical (ME)**: Practice Engineering Mechanics, SOM, TOM, CIM/Automation, Calculus/Linear Algebra.
2. **GATE Electrical (EE) / Instrumentation (IN)**: Practice Sensors/Transducers, Signal Conditioning, Control Systems, Op-Amps, Signals & Systems.
3. **GATE Data Science & AI (DA) / CS**: Practice Python programming, Data Structures, Linear Algebra, Probability & Statistics.
4. **GATE Production & Industrial (PI)**: Practice Automation, CNC, PLCs, Material Handling, AS/RS.
