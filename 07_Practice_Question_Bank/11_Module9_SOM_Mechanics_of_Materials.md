### SECTION A: STRESS, STRAIN & ELASTIC CONSTANTS — 25 Questions

**Q9.1 [NAT]** A bar of cross-section A=500mm², length L=1m, E=200GPa carries axial load P=100kN. Stress σ=?
> **Answer:** σ=P/A=100000/500×10⁻⁶=200 MPa.

**Q9.2 [MCQ]** Hooke's law: σ=Eε. For E=200GPa, ε=0.001: σ=?
- (A) 0.2 MPa  (B) 200 MPa  (C) 2 GPa  (D) 0.2 GPa
> **Answer: (B).** σ=200×10⁹×0.001=200×10⁶=200 MPa.

**Q9.3 [NAT]** Elastic constants relationship: E=2G(1+ν). For E=200GPa, ν=0.25: G=?
> **Answer:** G=E/[2(1+ν)]=200/[2(1.25)]=200/2.5=80 GPa.

**Q9.4 [MCQ]** Volumetric strain ev=σavg/K where K=bulk modulus=E/[3(1-2ν)]. For E=200GPa, ν=0.3: K=?
- (A) 100GPa  (B) 133.3GPa  (C) 200GPa  (D) 166.7GPa
> **Answer: (D).** K=200/[3(1-0.6)]=200/[3×0.4]=200/1.2=166.7 GPa.

**Q9.5 [NAT]** Relationship: E=3K(1-2ν). For K=167GPa, ν=0.3: E=?
> **Answer:** E=3×167×(1-0.6)=3×167×0.4=200.4≈200 GPa. ✓

**Q9.6 [MCQ]** Poisson's ratio ν for steel is approximately:
- (A) 0  (B) 0.5  (C) 0.3  (D) 1.0
> **Answer: (C).**

**Q9.7 [NAT]** A steel rod (E=200GPa, ν=0.3) under σx=200MPa, σy=σz=0. Longitudinal strain εx=?
> **Answer:** εx=σx/E=200×10⁶/(200×10⁹)=0.001=1000 με.

**Q9.8 [MCQ]** For uniaxial stress σx: transverse strain εy=?
- (A) -ν×εx  (B) ν×εx  (C) εx/E  (D) εx/ν
> **Answer: (A).** εy=-ν(σx/E)=-ν×εx.

**Q9.9 [NAT]** Volumetric strain for hydrostatic stress σ: ΔV/V=?
> **Answer:** ΔV/V=3σ(1-2ν)/E=σ/K.

**Q9.10 [MCQ]** Safety factor (FOS) in design:
- (A) FOS=applied stress/yield stress
- (B) FOS=yield stress/applied stress
- (C) FOS=fracture stress/elastic stress
- (D) FOS=UTS/yield stress
> **Answer: (B).** FOS=strength/working stress.

**Q9.11 [NAT]** Bar of steel: L=500mm, A=200mm², P=40kN, E=200GPa. Deformation δ=?
> **Answer:** δ=PL/(AE)=40000×0.5/(200×10⁻⁶×200×10⁹)=20000/(40000000)=5×10⁻⁴m=0.5mm.

**Q9.12 [MCQ]** True stress differs from engineering stress after:
- (A) Elastic deformation  (B) Necking (large plastic deformation)  (C) Fracture  (D) Yield point
> **Answer: (B).**

**Q9.13 [NAT]** Strain energy density u=σ²/(2E). For σ=100MPa, E=200GPa: u=?
> **Answer:** u=(100×10⁶)²/(2×200×10⁹)=10¹⁰/(4×10¹¹)=0.025 MJ/m³=25 kJ/m³.

**Q9.14 [MCQ]** Thermal stress in a constrained bar: σ=?
- (A) EαΔT  (B) αΔT/E  (C) EΔT  (D) Eα
> **Answer: (A).** Thermal stress σ=EαΔT (no free expansion allowed).

**Q9.15 [NAT]** Steel bar constrained at both ends, ΔT=50°C, α=12×10⁻⁶/°C, E=200GPa: thermal stress?
> **Answer:** σ=EαΔT=200×10⁹×12×10⁻⁶×50=120 MPa.

**Q9.16 [MCQ]** Factor that increases yield strength by cold working:
- (A) Annealing  (B) Strain hardening  (C) Quenching  (D) Normalizing
> **Answer: (B).**

**Q9.17 [NAT]** For biaxial stress (σx=100MPa, σy=50MPa, τxy=0): principal stresses?
> **Answer:** σ1=100 MPa, σ2=50 MPa (already principal since τxy=0).

**Q9.18 [MCQ]** Octahedral shear stress theory (von Mises) failure criterion: τoct=?
- (A) (σ1-σ2)/2  (B) (1/3)√[(σ1-σ2)²+(σ2-σ3)²+(σ3-σ1)²]
- (C) Max(σi)/2  (D) (σ1+σ2+σ3)/3
> **Answer: (B).**

**Q9.19 [NAT]** von Mises criterion for yielding (uniaxial): σ_e=σ1 at yield point when σ_e≥Syt. Von Mises equivalent stress for σx=100MPa, σy=0, τxy=60MPa?
> **Answer:** σ_e=√(σx²-σxσy+σy²+3τxy²)=√(10000+0+0+3×3600)=√(10000+10800)=√20800≈144.2 MPa.

**Q9.20 [MCQ]** Tresca (maximum shear stress) criterion: yielding when τmax≥?
- (A) Syt  (B) Syt/2  (C) 0.577Syt  (D) 2Syt
> **Answer: (B).** Tresca: τmax=Syt/2 at yield.

**Q9.21 [NAT]** Rankine (maximum normal stress) theory: failure when max principal stress=?
> **Answer:** Sut (ultimate tensile strength) — used for brittle materials.

**Q9.22 [MCQ]** Material with E=200GPa, Syt=250MPa. Allowable stress with FOS=2:
- (A) 500MPa  (B) 250MPa  (C) 125MPa  (D) 100MPa
> **Answer: (C).** Allowable=250/2=125 MPa.

**Q9.23 [NAT]** A shaft transmits power P=50kW at N=1000RPM. Torque T=?
> **Answer:** T=P/(2πN/60)=50000/(2π×1000/60)=50000/104.72=477.5 N·m.

**Q9.24 [MCQ]** Composite bar (two materials in series): compatible quantity is:
- (A) Stress (equal stresses)  (B) Force (equal forces)  (C) Strain (equal strains)  (D) Modulus
> **Answer: (B).** Series: same force P, different stresses and strains.

**Q9.25 [NAT]** Composite bar (two materials in parallel): compatible quantity?
> **Answer:** Deformation/strain (equal deformations); forces add up: P=P₁+P₂.

### SECTION B: MOHR'S CIRCLE & PRINCIPAL STRESSES — 20 Questions

**Q9.26 [NAT]** Mohr's circle center C for state (σx=100, σy=-40, τxy=48 MPa)?
> **Answer:** C=(σx+σy)/2=(100+(-40))/2=30 MPa.

**Q9.27 [MCQ]** Radius of Mohr's circle R=?
- (A) (σx-σy)/2  (B) √[(σx-σy)²/4+τxy²]  (C) τxy  (D) (σx+σy)/2
> **Answer: (B).**

**Q9.28 [NAT]** For σx=100, σy=-40, τxy=48 MPa: R=?
> **Answer:** R=√[(100-(-40))²/4+48²]=√[70²+48²]=√[4900+2304]=√7204≈84.9 MPa.

**Q9.29 [MCQ]** Principal stresses: σ1,2=C±R. For C=30, R=84.9 MPa: σ1=?
- (A) 54.9 MPa  (B) 114.9 MPa  (C) 84.9 MPa  (D) 100 MPa
> **Answer: (B).** σ1=30+84.9=114.9 MPa.

**Q9.30 [NAT]** Maximum in-plane shear stress τmax=?
> **Answer:** τmax=R=(σ1-σ2)/2=84.9 MPa (radius of Mohr's circle).

**Q9.31 [MCQ]** Angle to principal plane: tan(2θp)=?
- (A) 2τxy/(σx-σy)  (B) τxy/(σx-σy)  (C) (σx-σy)/2τxy  (D) 2τxy/(σx+σy)
> **Answer: (A).**

**Q9.32 [NAT]** For σx=80, σy=40, τxy=30 MPa: principal stresses σ1, σ2?
> **Answer:** C=60, R=√[20²+30²]=√(400+900)=√1300≈36.06. σ1=96.06, σ2=23.94 MPa.

**Q9.33 [MCQ]** At the principal planes: shear stress=?
- (A) Maximum  (B) Zero  (C) τxy  (D) R
> **Answer: (B).** Principal planes have zero shear stress.

**Q9.34 [NAT]** For σx=σy=50MPa, τxy=0: Mohr's circle is?
> **Answer:** A single point (degenerate circle with R=0). State of hydrostatic stress — all normal stresses equal.

**Q9.35 [MCQ]** Pure shear state (σx=σy=0, τxy=τ): principal stresses are:
- (A) 0 and 2τ  (B) τ and -τ  (C) τ and 0  (D) 2τ and 0
> **Answer: (B).** C=0, R=τ. σ1=τ, σ2=-τ.

**Q9.36 [NAT]** Absolute maximum shear stress (3D) for σ1=100MPa, σ2=60MPa, σ3=0?
> **Answer:** τabs_max=(σmax-σmin)/2=(100-0)/2=50 MPa.

**Q9.37 [MCQ]** The angle between principal plane and maximum shear plane is:
- (A) 0°  (B) 30°  (C) 45°  (D) 90°
> **Answer: (C).** Maximum shear planes are 45° from principal planes.

**Q9.38 [NAT]** For thin-walled pressure vessel: circumferential (hoop) stress σh=pd/(2t). For p=2MPa, d=500mm, t=5mm: σh=?
> **Answer:** σh=2×0.5/(2×0.005)=1.0/0.01=100 MPa.

**Q9.39 [MCQ]** Longitudinal stress in thin cylinder: σl=pd/(4t). Ratio σh/σl=?
- (A) 1  (B) 2  (C) 4  (D) 0.5
> **Answer: (B).** σh/σl=[pd/2t]/[pd/4t]=2.

**Q9.40 [NAT]** For thin sphere: σh=σl=pd/(4t). For p=1MPa, d=400mm, t=4mm: hoop stress?
> **Answer:** σh=1×0.4/(4×0.004)=0.4/0.016=25 MPa.

**Q9.41 [MCQ]** Thick cylinder (Lamé equations): maximum hoop stress occurs at:
- (A) Outer surface  (B) Inner surface  (C) Midwall  (D) Uniform throughout
> **Answer: (B).**

**Q9.42 [NAT]** Thin cylinder: volumetric strain εv=(pd/2tE)(5/2-2ν). For p=2MPa, d=200mm, t=4mm, E=200GPa, ν=0.3?
> **Answer:** εv=(2×0.2)/(2×0.004×200×10⁹)×(2.5-0.6)=(0.4/1.6×10⁹)×1.9=2.5×10⁻¹⁰×1.9×10⁰=... Simplified: εv=pd(5-4ν)/(4tE)=2×200×(5-1.2)/(4×4×200000)=400×3.8/3200000≈4.75×10⁻⁴.

**Q9.43 [MCQ]** In Mohr's circle, the point representing the state on a horizontal plane (σx,τxy) has coordinates:
- (A) (σx, τxy)  (B) (σy, -τxy)  (C) (σx, -τxy)  (D) (τxy, σx)
> **Answer: (A).** Convention: x-face point = (σx, τxy), y-face point = (σy, -τxy).

**Q9.44 [NAT]** For state σx=0, σy=0, τxy=50MPa: maximum normal stress?
> **Answer:** σmax=τxy=50 MPa (at 45° principal plane).

**Q9.45 [MCQ]** Failure of a ductile material under combined loading is best predicted by:
- (A) Rankine criterion  (B) Tresca or von Mises criterion  (C) Mohr's criterion  (D) Coulomb-Mohr
